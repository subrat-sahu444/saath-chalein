Safety & Security by Design

🪪
College verification

Access can be limited to approved students using institution-controlled verification.

🔐
Ride OTP

A one-time trip code helps confirm that the intended rider and passenger join the correct trip.

🚨
SOS support

An emergency action can surface trusted contacts and the college support channel during an active trip.

📍
Live trip sharing

Students can optionally share the active trip status/location with a trusted contact.

⚠
Report & block

Users can report unsafe driving, harassment or inappropriate behaviour and block accounts pending review.

🛡
Privacy controls

Location visibility should be limited to what is needed for matching and active-trip safety.

Pilot-first approach

Saath Chalein should start with one college, a limited number of verified users, clear pickup rules, a support process and measurable safety/usage metrics. The model can then be refined before expanding to other colleges.
